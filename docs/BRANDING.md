# Opoint Brand Usage

Opoint is the business operations and retail product within the Otya ecosystem. Keep the Opoint product identity distinct from the Otya parent brand.

## Approved source artwork

Use the exact source-derived artwork from the owner's approved Otya banner:

- Otya master lockup: https://assets.otya.site/brand/otya/v4/otya-master-lockup.webp
- Otya orbital mark: https://assets.otya.site/brand/otya/v4/otya-orbital-mark.webp
- Opoint product icon crop: https://assets.otya.site/brand/otya/v4/icons/opoint.webp

**Shop. Pay. More.**

## Usage rules

1. Use the Opoint product icon for Opoint app surfaces and the Otya master lockup for parent-brand placements.
2. Preserve the supplied artwork exactly. Do not redraw the logo, substitute fonts, recolour, alter gradients or invent product marks.
3. Public physical-shop branding uses **Opoint** as the shop name. Do not substitute OtyaPoint on shop signage or customer-facing shop materials.
4. Opoint remains a product within the Otya ecosystem; Otya Holdings Limited is the intended parent entity. Do not imply incorporation until registration is complete.
5. Do not use the full banner as an app icon. Use the exact Opoint product icon crop for compact product placements.
6. Keep the Otya parent palette and Opoint product identity consistent across web, mobile, email, documentation and receipts.

## Current implementation status

This repository currently documents the product and backend foundation. Apply these assets when the Opoint app and customer-facing surfaces are implemented. Do not claim the app UI has been branded until those surfaces exist and are verified.
